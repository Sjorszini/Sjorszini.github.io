Geometry tool

Finsler geometry calculator

Enter either a positively 2-homogeneous Finsler Lagrangian L(x,y), or a symmetric nondegenerate pseudo-Riemannian metric matrix gij(x). The calculator derives the canonical nonlinear connection, Christoffel symbols and curvature quantities symbolically.

Conventions follow Chapters 1–4 of Finsler Geometry, Spacetime & Gravity, in particular equations (1.4), (2.14), (2.32)–(2.34), (3.16), (3.23), and (4.3)–(4.4).

Input

Finsler Lagrangian

Use x1,…,xn and y1,…,yn. Syntax follows math.js: ^, sqrt(...), exp(...), sin(...), etc.

Enter a square symmetric matrix using double brackets. Entries may depend on x1,…,xn but not on y. Any nondegenerate signature is allowed.

Randers pp-wave: x1=u, x2=v, x3=x, x4=y; H=x3^2-x4^2 and β=du.

Results to compute / show

Unchecked result sections are not displayed. Quantities required as prerequisites for selected results may still be calculated internally.

Ready.

Calculations run in a background worker. Finished tensor components are shown immediately with timings. Very large expressions are collapsed initially and rendered only when you choose to expand them.
