Geometry tool

Finsler geometry calculator

Calculate connection and curvature quantities from a 2-homogeneous Finsler Lagrangian, a pseudo-Riemannian metric, or an α–β metric built from a metric and 1-form.

Conventions follow Finsler Geometry, Spacetime & Gravity. Lorentzian examples use the (-+++) signature convention.

Definitions and conventions used
\[g_{ij}=\frac12\bar\partial_i\bar\partial_j L\]
\[G^j=\frac12 g^{jk}\left(y^m\partial_m\bar\partial_k L-\partial_kL\right)\]
\[N^j{}_i=\frac12\bar\partial_iG^j,\qquad \delta_i=\partial_i-N^a{}_i\bar\partial_a\]
\[{}^B\Gamma^k{}_{ij}=\frac12\bar\partial_i\bar\partial_jG^k\]
\[{}^C\Gamma^k{}_{ij}=\frac12g^{k\ell}(\delta_i g_{\ell j}+\delta_jg_{i\ell}-\delta_\ell g_{ij})\]
\[R^k{}_{ij}=\delta_iN^k{}_j-\delta_jN^k{}_i\]
\[R^k{}_i=R^k{}_{ij}y^j,\qquad \mathrm{Ric}=R^i{}_{ij}y^j\]
\[R_{ij}=\frac12\bar\partial_i\bar\partial_j\mathrm{Ric}\]

Lorentzian presets and catalogue entries are presented in (-+++). A manual metric is used with the signature you enter.
