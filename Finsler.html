Geometry tool

Finsler geometry calculator

Enter a positively 2-homogeneous Finsler Lagrangian L(x,y). The calculator derives the canonical nonlinear connection, Berwald and Chern–Rund Christoffel symbols, nonlinear curvature, geodesic-deviation tensor and Finsler-Ricci quantities symbolically.

Conventions follow Chapters 1–4 of Finsler Geometry, Spacetime & Gravity, in particular equations (1.4), (2.14), (2.32)–(2.34), (3.16), (3.23), and (4.3)–(4.4).

Input

Finsler Lagrangian

Use x1,…,xn and y1,…,yn. Syntax follows math.js: ^, sqrt(...), exp(...), sin(...), etc.

Ready.

Quadratic/Riemannian inputs use a dedicated fast path. Non-quadratic Finsler expressions can still grow rapidly, especially in four dimensions; computing the connection first lets you inspect the intermediate geometry before requesting curvature.

Conventions used

\[g_{ij}=\frac12\,\bar\partial_i\bar\partial_j L\]
\[G^j=\frac12 g^{jk}\left(y^m\partial_m\bar\partial_k L-\partial_kL\right)\]
\[N^j{}_i=\frac12\bar\partial_iG^j,\qquad \delta_i=\partial_i-N^a{}_i\bar\partial_a\]
\[{}^B\Gamma^k{}_{ij}=\frac12\bar\partial_i\bar\partial_jG^k\]
\[{}^C\Gamma^k{}_{ij}=\frac12g^{k\ell}(\delta_i g_{\ell j}+\delta_j g_{i\ell}-\delta_\ell g_{ij})\]
\[R^k{}_{ij}=\delta_iN^k{}_j-\delta_jN^k{}_i\]
\[R^k{}_i=R^k{}_{ij}y^j,\qquad \mathrm{Ric}=R^i{}_{ij}y^j\]
\[R_{ij}=\frac12\bar\partial_i\bar\partial_j\mathrm{Ric}\]
