Mathematical physics · geometry · relativity

Lecturer and researcher in differential geometry and gravity.

I am a lecturer at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at Eindhoven University of Technology. My research focuses on differential geometry and relativity theory, particularly (pseudo-)Finsler geometry, modified theories of gravity, and connections with quantum gravity.

I am also a management committee member of COST Action CA23130, Bridging High and Low Energies in Search of Quantum Gravity (BridgeQG). My publications and preprints are collected on the publications page.

Outside academia, I am a pianist and band leader for Gospel/Pop Choir Spirit. More information is available on the pianist page.
